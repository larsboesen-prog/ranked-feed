MapSMA /page5: MapSMA's ears and MapSMA's voice, English and Spanish (21 Nov 2026)
=================================================================================

What this changes
-----------------
/page5 (Ask by voice):
- hears questions with MapSMA's ears (8.9 MB, both languages, knows San Miguel's names) instead of Whisper tiny (about 40 MB);
  Whisper tiny stays a choice in Voice settings, and is used by itself if the ears cannot load;
- reads answers with MapSMA's voice (MX1, the warm local guide, 8 MB) in Spanish and in English; the phone's own voice stays a
  choice, with the fixes for iPhones (silent switch, late voices);
- marks our picks "★ Our pick" (no more "Not our pick"), with descriptions for the extra places (/descs.js);
- the credit line for the ears (CC BY-SA 4.0) and the voice (tiny-voca) in Voice settings.
First visit: about 30 MB instead of about 50-75 MB; afterwards the phone keeps the files.

Upload
------
1. Copy everything inside the folder mapsma.com/ to the site's web root, keeping the folders:
     page5/index.html, ask.js, words.js, rules.js, susan.js   (replace the old ones)
     descs.js                                                  (new, at the root, next to places.js)
     tiny-speech/                                              (new folder, about 18 MB; the shared core, models included)
   Nothing else on the site changes. /page5's old asr-worker.js, tinyvoca-worker.js and tinyvoca/ can stay (no longer used).
2. The site's server (nginx) needs nothing special: .js as JavaScript (the default), .onnx and .gguf as files (the default).
   onnxruntime-web comes from cdn.jsdelivr.net, as the voice's engine files did before.

Check on the iPhone (and a computer)
------------------------------------
1. Open https://mapsma.com/page5/ , English. Voice settings: "Hear questions with" says "MapSMA's ears"; tap Test the voice:
   MapSMA's voice speaks English.
2. Ask by voice: "Is La Azotea open now?" The words appear (in small letters, no punctuation), then the answer, read aloud.
3. Switch to ES. Ask: "¿Está abierta La Azotea?" and "¿Dónde hay música en vivo esta noche?" The answers are read in
   Spanish by the same voice.
4. Note how long the first answer takes the first time (the files download once) and the second time.
5. With the side switch on silent: MapSMA's voice still speaks (it plays as media).
Optional: https://mapsma.com/tiny-speech/browser/check.html (Load, Record, round trip) tests the parts one by one.

Going back
----------
Put the files in backup/page5/ back into the site's page5/ (they are the live files as served since 18 Nov 2026).
/descs.js and /tiny-speech/ can stay; nothing else uses them.
